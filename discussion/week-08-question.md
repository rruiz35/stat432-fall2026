Suppose two classes are linearly separable, and you fit a soft-margin linear SVM with a very large cost $C$ and then refit it with a much smaller $C$. Does the fitted boundary have to change, and which observations determine it in each case? Explain how the slack variables and the dual coefficients shape your answer.
