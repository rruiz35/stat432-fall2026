Ridge regression adds a penalty to ordinary least squares so that coefficient estimates shrink toward zero. In a setting with predictors that are almost redundant, why might OLS produce unstable coefficient estimates? How does ridge regression reduce that instability by shrinking the slope estimates, and why can this lower prediction variance even if it introduces a small amount of bias? Explain the bias-variance tradeoff in plain language and relate it to the idea of effective degrees of freedom from Week 2.
